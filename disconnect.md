# Disconnect QuickBooks Online

**Miami Independent Learners, LLC — Kumon Miami Shores Integration**

## Disconnecting Your QuickBooks Account

This integration is intended for authorized internal business use only.

To request disconnection of a QuickBooks Online company from this application, contact the application administrator.

The administrator will revoke the application's QuickBooks authorization and remove the stored connection credentials.

## What Happens After Disconnection

- The application will no longer be authorized to retrieve new QuickBooks information.
- Previously retrieved business records may be retained according to the company's applicable data retention policies.
- Reconnecting will require a new authorization through Intuit QuickBooks Online.

## Security

Disconnection requests must be verified by an authorized company administrator to prevent unauthorized changes to the accounting integration.
