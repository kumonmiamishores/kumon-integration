
# Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks Online

**Miami Independent Learners, LLC — Kumon Miami Shores Integration**

This integration is intended exclusively for authorized company administrators.

## Connecting QuickBooks

To connect or reconnect the integration to QuickBooks Online, contact the application administrator.

The administrator will initiate the secure authorization process through Intuit QuickBooks Online.

You will be asked to sign in to Intuit and authorize access to the appropriate QuickBooks company.

## Security

- QuickBooks passwords are never collected by this website.
- Authorization is processed through Intuit.
- Connection credentials are managed securely by the application's backend.
