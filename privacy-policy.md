# Privacy Policy
**Miami Independent Learners, LLC — Kumon Miami Shores Integration**

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Miami Independent Learners, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates an internal software integration supporting administrative activities associated with its Kumon learning centers.

This Privacy Policy describes how our integration accesses, uses, stores, and protects information obtained through QuickBooks Online and related business systems.

This integration is intended for authorized internal business use and is not a public consumer application.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the features enabled, our integration may access:

- QuickBooks customer names and customer identifiers.
- Invoice information, including amounts, dates, balances, and payment status.
- Payment records and their relationships to invoices.
- Business account identifiers and technical information required to maintain the connection.

The integration is not intended to access banking credentials or credit card numbers.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is accessed for legitimate internal administrative purposes, including:

- Reconciling customer accounts and payments.
- Reviewing outstanding invoices and balances.
- Supporting authorized business reporting.
- Connecting approved administrative systems.

We do not sell QuickBooks information or use it for advertising.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Our integration uses Supabase-hosted backend services to communicate with QuickBooks Online.

We use access controls and encrypted storage for QuickBooks authorization tokens. Access to the integration is intended to be restricted to authorized personnel.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect business information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.

## 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent information obtained through QuickBooks Online.

Information may be processed by service providers supporting the integration, including Intuit and Supabase, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may disclose information when legally required.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the integration's legitimate business purposes, applicable legal requirements, and operational needs.

Authorized administrators may discontinue the QuickBooks connection. Upon disconnection, stored authorization credentials should be revoked or deleted, subject to applicable retention obligations.

## 7. Children's Information

Our learning centers serve children, and some business records may relate to their parents or guardians.

This integration is intended for administrative and accounting purposes. It is not designed for direct use by children.

Student attendance records and student photographs are outside the intended scope of the QuickBooks accounting integration.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Our integration may use:

- **Intuit QuickBooks Online** for accounting information.
- **Supabase** for backend infrastructure and secure data processing.

Each provider maintains its own privacy and security practices.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our integration, business practices, or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page.
