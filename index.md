# Kumon Miami Shores Integration

**Operated by Miami Independent Learners, LLC**

This website provides information about our internal business software integration with QuickBooks Online.

The integration supports authorized administrative activities, including reviewing customer accounts, invoices, payments, and outstanding balances.

The application is intended for internal business use only and is not available for public registration.

## QuickBooks Integration

Access to QuickBooks Online requires authorization by an approved company administrator.

For assistance with connecting, reconnecting, or disconnecting the integration, please contact the application administrator.
